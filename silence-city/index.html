Silence City

by Shrekizzler Production

An arcade game about smashing fictional ads: tear down billboards, pop pop-ups, find the real cross on fake banners and earn “silence” to clean up the city. 1000 levels, seven districts, daily quests, an endless mode and a sandbox.

All brands in the game are invented. The game does not block ads in other apps.
